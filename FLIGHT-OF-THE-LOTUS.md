# Botus 1-2-3: the unnecessary manual supplement

Recalculate like it is 1989. Budget your context like you paid for the RAM.

## Welcome to the spreadsheet department

- One workbook. Two tools. Three numbers in the name. We have already
  exceeded the original business plan.
- Your agent has discovered a shared message board. Finance has discovered
  that the message board has columns. Finance is taking over.
- Please enter your expenses in column B. Existential questions go in C.
- The agent in A1 has found other agents. The agent in A2 wants a SUM.
- All rights reserved. Some ranges named.

## Installation

- Insert Disk 1. Remember that there is no disk. Register the optional bundle.
- The package is `botus-1-2-3`. Please spell all three numbers when requesting
  it from the purchasing department.
- No autoexec.bat changes are required. Your lunch break is safe.
- The beige computer is sold separately. The beige budget is included.
- Expanded memory is useful. An expanded tool list is not included.

## Operation

- The slash menu has been condensed into two tools. Somewhere, a laminated
  keyboard reference card is having a difficult morning.
- Recalculation is automatic. Your F9 key may finally pursue other interests.
- Formula cells are explicit. A string beginning with an equals sign has
  not accidentally been promoted to middle management.
- A named range is a range with a name. This is the entire branding strategy.
- Copying formulas adjusts references. Copying your manager on every message
  remains outside the product specification.
- An empty cell is not an empty string. The accounts department insists on
  keeping these two forms of nothing separate.
- Read a bounded range. The whole spreadsheet would like to fit in your
  context window, but it has brought its entire extended family.

## Networking, now with actual permission checks

- A fork gets permitted access to the same workbook. It does not inherit the
  corner office or the right to hand out keys.
- Sharing with descendants is explicit. A family tree is not an access list.
- Reusing an old worker's name does not unlock its filing cabinet. We checked
  this with the Department of Suspiciously Familiar Colleagues.
- Realm ownership is not public access. The word "global" is not a password.
- A retry uses the same request ID. Please do not buy the same floppy twice
  because the receipt printer was quiet.

## Compatibility and support

- Release 2.2 is an inspiration, not a binary compatibility claim.
- WK1 has been referred to the Committee for Jokes That Require Too Much
  Engineering. Its decision is to use versioned JSON for now.
- Printer drivers are unavailable. The imaginary tractor-feed paper remains
  securely in its imaginary box.
- Macros are not supported. Neither are spreadsheet viruses with excellent
  period authenticity.
- If the process restarts, consult the host's durable storage. If the host
  has no durable storage, installation refuses to pretend that it does.
- Save early, share deliberately, and keep the jokes out of the hot path.

For actual instructions, return to [the manual](USAGE.md) or
[the host integration guide](NATIVE.md). Pressing Escape is optional.
